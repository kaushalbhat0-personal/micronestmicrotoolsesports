League of Legends Patch 26.20 Notes

Game Updates
2026-10-06T18:00:00.000Z

Bee wee woop - it's time for WORLDS! Lock in, because we've got some adjustments to pro staples like Ambessa, and buffs to weaker champions who might be on the edge of viability like Kennen and Kindred. Rocketbelt is getting another tap to make it a bit worse as a purchasable Flash.

Austin "Riot wroadie" Wright

Patch Highlights

Prestige HEARTSTEEL Live My Life Sett will be available October 7, 2026.

Champions

Ashe

Ashe is outputting too much raw damage given her utility, so we're tapping her growth.

Base Stats

  • Attack Damage per Level: 3.5 ⇒ 3

Diana

We want to inject a bit more power into her bruiser playstyle.

W - Pale Cascade

  • Shield Bonus Health Ratio: 11% ⇒ 14%

Vayne

We're adjusting her base stats to help in bot and reverting a mana cost that felt way too bad.

Base Stats

  • Health per 5: 4 + .5/Level ⇒ 5.5 +.4/Level

Q - Tumble

  • Mana Cost: 46 / 42 / 38 / 34 / 30 ⇒ 30

K'Sante

Warding off this warden from being as reliable as a blind pick in coordinated play.

W - Path Maker

  • Mana Cost: 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60 ⇒ 45 / 50 / 55 / 60 / 65
  • W Base/All-Out Damage Reduction: 30 / 75% ⇒ 25 / 70%

Kindred

Kindred is currently a bit weak, so we're giving them buffs that won't make early invades overwhelming.

W - Wolf's Frenzy

  • Monster Mod: 150% ⇒ 165%

E - Mounting Dread

  • Slow Duration: 1s ⇒ 1.5s
  • Small Monster Targeting: Enabled ⇒ Removed

Nautilus

He's weak in all three of his main roles, so we're fixing one major bug while pulling in buffs.

Passive - Staggering Blow

  • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where the empowered attack was critting for too much damage.

W - Titan's Wrath

  • Damage over Time: 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 / 100 + 40% AP ⇒ 40 / 65 / 90 / 115 / 140 + 40% AP
  • Shield Duration: 9.75 ⇒ 10

Items

Hextech Rocketbelt

Rocketbelt is used too much as an escape, so we are pulling back its generic value.

  • Ability Haste: 20 ⇒ 10
  • Active Damage: 100 (+10% AP) ⇒ 135 (+15% AP)

Scoreboard Cleanup

Our recent systemic buff-bar clarity work moved numbers onto items. These numbers began terrorizing your scoreboards. The following items' icon counters are now hidden on the scoreboard:

  • Death's Dance
  • Haunting Guise

ARAM: Mayhem

Hello Mayhemers! We're giving some champions that need a little love some extra attention.

Champions

Bard

  • Meep Ability Power Ratio: 50% ⇒ 67%
  • Meep Recharge: 8s ⇒ 5s

Garen

  • W Cooldown: 22-12 ⇒ 18-12

Bugfixes & QoL Changes

  • Fixed a bug where Orianna's (R) VFX and actual hitbox become misplaced if she casts (R) before the ball reaches a fast-moving ally like Nocturne.
  • Fixed a bug where a Sylas-stolen Depth Charge (R) from Nautilus could fizzle after Sylas lost vision of the target.

Upcoming Skins & Chromas

The following skins will be released in this patch:

Prestige HEARTSTEEL Live My Life Sett

HEARTSTEEL Live My Life Ezreal
