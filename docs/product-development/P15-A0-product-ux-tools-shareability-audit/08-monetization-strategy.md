# 08 — Monetization Strategy (Realistic)

No revenue forecasts or user counts are invented. Assess fit against self-serve delivery and low operator support burden.

## Free acquisition channels (keep free)

| Asset | Who benefits | Why free |
|---|---|---|
| Prize / Roster / Tie-Break core | Organizers, managers, communities | Acquisition + watermark distribution |
| Opt-in player/team directories | Players, teams, orgs, brands | Network effects require density |
| LFT / recruiting PNG (basic) | Players/teams | Growth loop |

## Opportunities

### 1) Premium tool features

| Pays | Problem | Recurring? | Self-serve? | Support burden | Prove first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power organizers / managers | Multi-scenario packs, PDF packs, team workspaces, saved cloud scenarios | Likely seasonal | Yes if checkout automated | Medium (billing edge cases) | Share Kit usage + repeat tool sessions |

**Fit:** Medium. Do not gate core calculators early.

### 2) Premium player / team presentation

| Pays | Problem | Recurring? | Self-serve? | Support | Prove first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ambitious players/teams | Featured placement, richer card themes, pinned LFT | Monthly possible | Yes | Low–medium (content abuse) | Organic share volume |

**Constraint:** Must not sell fake “verification.” Premium ≠ verified.

### 3) Advanced roster / recruitment tools

| Pays | Problem | Recurring? | Self-serve? | Support | Prove first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teams / orgs | Open-slot posts, applicant inbox, CSV imports | During seasons | Partial (moderation) | **High** | Recruiting boolean demand |

**Fit:** Later — schema + authz heavy.

### 4) Organizer-focused tools

| Pays | Problem | Recurring? | Self-serve? | Support | Prove first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tournament organizers | Bracket ops, payout planning packs, multi-stage tiebreak | Per event / sub | Yes for digital goods | Medium | Tool PNG adoption |

Aligns with existing prize/tiebreak strengths.

### 5) Sponsored placements

| Pays | Problem | Recurring? | Self-serve? | Support | Prove first |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brands / orgs | Reach BGMI audience in directory/tools | Campaign | Needs sales **or** self-serve slots | High if manual | Traffic + honest inventory |

**Conflict risk:** Operator prefers async work — prefer productized slots over bespoke deals initially.

### 6) Affiliates

| Pays | Problem | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Peripheral vendors (peripherals, hosts) | Relevant offers | Only with clear disclosure; low priority vs core utility |

## What must be proven before charging

1. Users repeatedly open tools without prompting.
2. Users voluntarily share watermarked outputs.
3. Directory density is non-trivial in target regions.
4. Checkout + entitlement delivery can be automated (legacy billing existed; new platform has no live entitlements for tools — **MISSING**).

## Finding — F-BIZ-01

- Evidence: tools free + client-only; no entitlements in new catalog; legacy paid tools are reference-only.
- User impact: none today (everything free).
- Business impact: monetization premature until share loop works.
- Complexity: high for full billing revival. Risk: medium. Action: defer charging; instrument qualitative share success after M1.
