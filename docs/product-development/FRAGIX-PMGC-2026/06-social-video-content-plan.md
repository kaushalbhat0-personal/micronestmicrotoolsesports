# 06 — Social video content plan

Practical **15–30s** Reels / Shorts based on **actual** product screens (signup, settings, ownership states, public profile, PNG download). No fabricated stats. No official PMGC assets.

## Shared rules

- On-screen: “Unofficial community · Not affiliated with official PMGC”
- Never show BGMI Player ID digits, evidence screenshots, or reviewer notes
- Prefer demo/sample handles or consented creators
- End CTA: FRAGIX campaign URL (post brand cut) → Sign up

## Concept A — “From zero to card” (20–25s)

| Time | Shot (product) | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Dark Arena home / campaign hero | Hook: “Your BGMI season identity card” |
| 3–7s | Signup | “Create FRAGIX account” |
| 7–12s | Onboarding / settings profile | “Add IGN + profile” |
| 12–16s | BGMI ID field (blurred value) | “Save Player ID (private)” |
| 16–22s | Public profile + Download PNG | “Share your card” |
| 22–25s | PNG full frame | CTA: link in bio |

**Caption:** Build your community player card on FRAGIX. Unofficial PMGC 2026 community toolset — not an official event partner.  
**Hook variants:** “Stop sending boring screenshots.” / “One card. Your IGN. Your link.”

## Concept B — “Reviewed, not claimed” (20–30s)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Settings “Pending manual review” | “Submitted ownership evidence” |
| 4–10s | (No evidence pixels) abstract UI only | “Private review — screenshots stay private” |
| 10–18s | Public profile badge “BGMI ownership evidence reviewed” | Truthful label only |
| 18–25s | Share PNG download | “Then share the card” |
| 25–30s | Disclaimer card | “Not official BGMI / PMGC verification” |

**Caption:** Manual review. Honest badge. Private evidence.  
**Avoid:** “Get verified by BGMI”, checkmark-of-authority tropes.

## Concept C — “Tools that don’t lie” (15–20s) — secondary

Use existing tool share exports (prize/roster/tiebreak evidence PNGs) as B-roll **after** identity CTA. Reinforce independence: “Simulations aren’t official results” (already in tool disclaimers).

## Production tips

- Record from mobile Chrome/Safari against staging or local with demo account.
- Reuse stills from `P15-M1-share-kit-v1/evidence/` for cutaways until FRAGIX brand assets ship.
- Burned-in URL only after FRAGIX domain/path is decided.
