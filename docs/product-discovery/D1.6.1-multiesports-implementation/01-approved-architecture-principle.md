# 01 — Approved Architecture Principle (APPROVED 2026-11-11)

## Exact approved wording

"MicroNest Esports launches with BGMI as its first supported game, while its
identity, discovery, team and organization architecture is designed to support
multiple esports titles. New games will be introduced incrementally through
game-specific catalogues, profiles, roles and validation rules without changing
the platform-wide identity system."

## Approved implications (part of the approval)

- BGMI is the initial launch title.
- Multiple esports titles are the long-term direction.
- The architecture must be multi-title-ready.
- New games arrive incrementally.
- Platform-wide identity is separate from game-specific competitive identity.
- Team rosters and competitive attributes are game-specific.
- Organizations may eventually manage cross-game teams, subject to a separately
  approved authority model.

## Approval boundary (what it does NOT do)

- Does not approve D1-D1…D1-D12 (all remain AWAITING).
- Does not approve D1-D2's one-game-per-team rule (proposed, unapproved).
- Does not approve D1-D5's detailed vocabulary/validation contract.
- Does not approve org authority, captain authority, transfer, or lifecycle.
- Does not authorize seeding any game, migration application, or replay.
