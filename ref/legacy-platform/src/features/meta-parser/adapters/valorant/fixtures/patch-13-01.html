VALORANT Patch Notes 13.01

Hey, it's Ashley! Here's the breakdown for Patch 13.01:

  • Iso buff to weapon equip speed after unequipping Double Tap
  • Yoru buff with an increase in duration of Gatecrash
  • Outlaw adjustment to allow for more expression and control
  • Loads of bug fixes

ALL PLATFORMS

AGENTS UPDATES

  • Iso
    • Double Tap
      • The speed of equipping your weapon after unequipping Double Tap is now instant.
  • Yoru
    • Gatecrash
      • Active Beacon Duration increased from 15s >>> 20s
    • Fakeout
      • To further satisfy his deception fantasy, clones from Fakeout will now hold Yoru's last equipped weapon, instead of defaulting to his strongest weapon.

WEAPONS UPDATES

Outlaw has been performing strongly and we still want it to feel powerful.

  • Outlaw
    • Recovery from 0.1 >>> 0.15 after first shot
    • Spread from 0 >>> 2.25 after first shot
    • Recoil from 0 >>> 4.0 after first shot

BUG FIXES

  • Astra
    • Fixed an issue where Astra's ability cooldown timer could sometimes remain visible after the ability had already been used.
  • Cypher
  • Fixed an issue where Cyber Cage could incorrectly block line-of-sight interactions above the ability's visual effects.
  • Jett
    • Fixed an inconsistency where Jett could not pick up the Spike or weapons during Tailwind.

BUG FIXES

  • General
    • Fixed a cosmetic issue where Packets Received/Sent Rate graphs showed incorrect values when the game is running at extremely high framerates

KNOWN ISSUES

  • Omen
    • Shrouded Step's obstructed placement indicator is missing
