League of Legends Patch 26.19 Notes

Game Updates
2026-09-22T18:00:00.000Z

Our first round of pro-focused Summoner's Rift changes are coming up here. We've identified fringe picks for the tournament that could use a nudge up in power, and knocking some individual meta outliers as well (and also Nasus).

Riot wroadie, Riot Cashmiir, sternest

Patch Highlights

Coven Janna, Coven Mel, Old God Anivia, Old God Urgot will be available September 23, 2026.

Champions

Nasus

Nasus is difficult to keep down these days, so we're compensating higher highs with lower lows early on.

Q - Siphoning Strike

  • Bonus Damage: 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 ⇒ 30 / 50 / 70 / 90 / 110

Rumble

We've seen Rumble take over games at higher levels of play, so we're making modifications skewed toward elite audiences.

Passive - Junkyard Titan

  • Overheat Attack Speed: 50 - 130% ⇒ 30 - 100%
  • Monster Cap: 65 - 150 ⇒ 90 - 175

Vi

Vi has been a meta staple all year, so we're going after her durability and early clear speed.

Base Stats

  • Attack Damage: 63 + 3.5/Level ⇒ 61 + 3.9/Level

Graves

Graves came out swinging, so we're preserving his bursty shotgunning while reducing sustained damage.

R - Collateral Damage

  • Cone Damage: 140 / 250 / 360 + 120% bonus AD ⇒ 140 / 230 / 320 + 100% bonus AD
  • Projectile Damage: Unchanged

Items

World Atlas and Runic Compass

We think melee supports have too much power in roaming, so we're shifting flat health to health regen.

  • Health: 30 / 100 / 200 ⇒ 0 / 60 / 200
  • Health Regeneration: 25% / 50% / 75% ⇒ 50% / 75% / 75%

Systems

Teleport Cooldown

Top lane is slightly weak for split pushing, so we're buffing both Teleport variants.

  • Top Role Quest's Free Teleport Cooldown: 420 seconds ⇒ 390 seconds
  • Non-Top Unleashed Teleport Cooldown: 330 - 240 seconds ⇒ Unchanged

Classic

We've got our third big champion drop this patch with four fresh faces and the highlight is Graves!

Champions

NEW Graves

Welcome back, pre-rework Graves! This version from 2011-2015 is a true-to-live bot lane ADC.

Passive - True Grit: While in combat, Graves gains stacking Armor and Magic Resist every second, up to a cap.

Akali

Akali appears weak, so we're swinging by for some buffs to her energy efficiency.

E - Crescent Slash

  • Energy Cost: 60 / 60 / 60 / 60 / 60 ⇒ 60 / 55 / 50 / 45 / 40
  • Cooldown: 7 / 6 / 5 / 4 / 3s ⇒ 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1s

Arena

We've got something special for this Arena patch with three Guests of Honor and fresh Augments.

Champions

Aphelios

Base Stats

  • Health Growth: 102 ⇒ 114
  • Move Speed: 325 ⇒ 330

Bugfixes & QoL Changes

  • Fixed a bug where Renata's R would not amp damage caused by Berserk targets.
  • Fixed a bug where Taliyah's W terrain was smaller than the hitbox.
